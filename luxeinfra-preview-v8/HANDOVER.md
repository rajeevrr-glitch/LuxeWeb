# LuxeInfra - version 8

Prepared 17 October 2026. Responds to the request for a premium interior-studio experience with less emphasis on submitting forms.

## Design changes

- The homepage now leads into a spacious, staggered room collection. Large photographs replace the early row of project-intake cards.
- The first action is Explore the collection, followed by Meet the studio. Repeated floor-plan and process prompts were removed from the homepage; project planning remains available through Start your project in the footer, the contact page and existing tool routes.
- A short studio point of view replaces the procedural grid.
- Our process is rebuilt as a visual editorial page with a panoramic opening and three illustrated chapters: understanding everyday life, developing the design, and execution/handover.
- Practical stage details expand on demand. The quotation checklist remains functional inside a closed practical-details section.
- The shared header uses a quiet Contact studio link instead of a filled Plan my space button.
- Ivory, deep blue, serif typography and the existing photography remain the visual foundation. Images are labelled as design references rather than completed client projects.

## Checks

Desktop and 390px mobile layouts were visually reviewed. No horizontal overflow was observed on the checked mobile pages, including the expanded practical section. Chapter expansion and the quotation checklist counter were checked in the browser. TypeScript and production build both passed. The unchanged backend tests were not rerun for this presentation-only update; all 13 passed in version 7.

This is a presentation change. The AI provider remains disconnected. No enquiry was submitted or sent externally during this update. Nothing was published or pushed, and no GoDaddy or Lovable setting changed.
