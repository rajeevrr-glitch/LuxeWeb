# LuxeInfra - version 9

Prepared 17 October 2026. Builds on version 8 with a fuller visual treatment of Our process.

## What changed

- A compact opening combines the headline, room photograph, inset bedroom image, studio signature and floating editorial note.
- A blue three-part journey strip links directly to each process chapter.
- A new interactive palette study offers Warm & grounded, Soft & serene and Classic & composed. Selecting a direction updates its reference image, three named colour swatches and mood description. No form, AI call or data submission is involved.
- The introduction now uses two columns. Chapter spacing and image proportions are tighter, and the closing invitation has a warm neutral background.
- The ivory and deep-blue theme is preserved, with clear inspiration labels and optional practical tools.
